## 2. Scope of this policy

The {brand} wholesale showroom is a service for business customers (B2B): a collection catalogue, buyer accounts, PDF line sheets and contact with a sales representative. The showroom does not process sales or payments and we do not send marketing e-mails — we only send messages about your account.

## 3. What data we process, why and on what legal basis

### Buyer account

- **Data:** e-mail address, password (stored only as a cryptographic hash — we do not know your password), company name, country, tax ID / EU VAT number, contact person's name, phone number, language, account status, dates of registration, e-mail confirmation, account decision and last sign-in, failed sign-in counter.
- **Purpose:** creating and maintaining the account, checking that the applicant is a wholesale customer, providing wholesale prices and line sheets, contacting you about cooperation.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract — the showroom terms of service) and, for the data of the contact person acting on behalf of the company, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in verifying business partners and in business communication).

### PDF line sheets

- **Data:** company and contact details from the account at the moment the line sheet is generated (stored with the line sheet), selected products and indicative quantities, assigned sales representative, the PDF file.
- **Purpose:** preparing the line sheet at your request and handling your enquiry by a sales representative.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### Record of accepted documents

- **Data:** which document (terms of service, privacy policy), which version, when, in which language and in what situation (registration, new document version) it was accepted or acknowledged. We do not store IP addresses or browser data.
- **Purpose:** demonstrating the terms on which the contract was concluded and that we met our information obligation.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 5(2) GDPR (accountability) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

### Security and session

- The session cookie `showroom_buyer_refresh` (not accessible to scripts, valid for up to 30 days) keeps you signed in; we store only its hash in the database. A short-lived access token (15 minutes) exists only in the memory of the browser tab.
- We limit the number of requests from a single address (e.g. sign-in attempts). The IP address is used for this only in server memory and is not stored in the database or in statistics.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security of the service and accounts).

### Visit statistics

- **Without your consent** we count only anonymous events (page or product view, adding a product to a line sheet, clicking contact details) — without identifiers, IP addresses or browser data. Nothing is stored on your device.
- **With your consent** (privacy banner) we store a random visitor identifier (`fs-visitor`, renewed after 13 months) and a visit identifier (`fs-session`, until the tab is closed) in your browser, which let us count unique visitors.
- You can withdraw your consent at any time under “Privacy settings” in the page footer — the identifiers are then removed from your browser.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) and the rules on storing information on the user's device [legal review: applicable national provision].

### Contact with a sales representative

When you write to or call a sales representative, we process the data from that correspondence to reply and to conduct business talks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 4. Information stored in your browser

- `showroom_buyer_refresh` — session cookie of a signed-in buyer (strictly necessary).
- `fs-buyer-session` — marker that a buyer is signed in on this device (strictly necessary).
- `fs-line-sheet` — your product selection for a line sheet, without prices (necessary for the feature you use).
- `fs-consent` — your decision about statistics, valid for 12 months (strictly necessary).
- `fs-visitor`, `fs-session` — statistics identifiers, only with your consent.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking tools.
